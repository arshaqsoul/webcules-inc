● Production at Villvay

Villvay Search Platform

Multi-tenant B2B e-commerce search for Wurth industrial suppliers — ~35k lines of TypeScript and Python, 5 tenants isolated end to end.

  • 16-layer relevance cascade from exact SKU to fuzzy, with tier suppression at result thresholds.
  • Weighted Reciprocal Rank Fusion across 11 retrievers; ELSER sparse-vector semantic fallback computed at ingest, so query time pays zero inference.
  • E-commerce linguistics: unit-of-measure synonyms, fraction/dimension normalization, per-account part numbers, barcode lookup.
  • Zero-code tenant onboarding: config-only, 11 routes and 10 search collections per tenant.
  • Airflow pipelines — one templated DAG generating per-tenant DAGs — syncing on 4-hour schedules with watermark incrementals.
  • Config platform: 33 tRPC procedures, draft → preview → promote, one-click rollback.

ELUX Travels

Multi-region travel booking platform — tours, hotels, flights, cruises — designed, built and shipped solo.

  • ~46k lines of hand-written TypeScript, 23 routes, 22 CMS collections, 42 server actions, 460+ commits over an 11-month build.
  • Next.js + Payload CMS 3 running as Cloudflare Workers via OpenNext, with edge ISR (R2 incremental cache, D1 tag cache, Durable Object queue).

● Design study

AI Oncology Capstone

AI-assisted breast cancer early-detection and diagnostics module designed for the COSMIC EHR, in the Sri Lankan context.

  • Full research → synthesis → ideation → POC design sprint, aligned to Cambio's roadmap and the EU Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI.
  • Three concepts evaluated through a weighted Harris Profile; selected a CNN clinician-assistant concept over a patient app and a risk model.
  • Measurable AI requirements as design targets: 80%+ detection accuracy, 60% faster lab-investigation selection, ≤5 minutes screen time per consultation.
  • Clinician-in-the-loop second opinions designed to discourage over-requisitioning invasive tests; anonymized scan-image repository proposed for AI development.
