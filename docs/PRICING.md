## Webcules price list (CAD, tax extra)

### Add-ons (stack on top of the tier)

| Add-on | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Online ordering / menu ordering / e-commerce-lite | +$400 | product/menu list, no full inventory systems |
| Booking / appointment flow or 3rd-party embed wiring | +$200 | Cal.com, Setmore, etc. |
| Copywriting from scratch (site had nothing usable) | +$150 | usually not needed — substance is preserved |
| Rush (live in 48 h) | +$200 | only when actually rushed |
| Extra generated art beyond the standard set | +$100 | hero video etc. — usually included, charge only when they ask for more |

### Installments — split the total, max 6 months

Same total, no fee, no interest: **$499 → 5 × $100 · $799 → 6 × $133 · $1,199 → 6 × $200 · $1,999 → 6 × $333.** Months scale with the amount (~$100/mo minimum payment), capped at 6. In every pitch, write it exactly like this: **"$799 CAD (or $133 CAD/month × 6)"** — one line, both options, no fine print. Upfront always gets a small nudge ("live this week") but never a discount for paying early.

### Maintenance — $10/month, cancel anytime

Hosting (Cloudflare), domain renewal handling, content/price/menu edits within 48 h, uptime + AI-readiness check monthly, quarterly refresh of seasonal content. Market equivalent: $40–$165/mo. **Never discount the one-time price without removing scope; flex on payment (installments instead of upfront) instead.**

## Rules

1. Prices end in 99. Quoted price is what gtm.json carries — never invent a different number in an email.
2. Always quote **with** the market range next to it. The price only makes sense against $2,500+.
3. Payment: upfront or monthly installments (max 6 months, same total). Stripe checkout link (card) or Interac e-Transfer (most Saskatoon small businesses).
4. Domain switch, DNS, and go-live are **included** — say so explicitly, it removes the scariest part for owners.
5. If a prospect pushes below $499, they're a maintenance-only lead: keep the preview, offer $10/mo + they cover launch at $299. Otherwise walk politely.
6. Review this file every ~6 months or when 3 prospects in a row say "yes" instantly (price too low — raise the tier floor by $100).
