# Storyboard: PP-014 - The photographer's brand lives in one logo field and nowhere else

Pain point: PP-014
Persona: brand-conscious wedding photographer whose logo sits in one setting while everything else ships generic
Tier shown: studio
Format: desktop
Seed: 14
Target length: 20-30 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): One logo, everywhere
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is Settings - Brand with the logo drop zone, and the promise is that this one file becomes every touchpoint.
No logo in the first frame.

## Starting state

Fixture to seed (through the app UI and the app's own APIs, nothing by hand in the database):
- The demo org "Amara & Oak Photography" on the Studio plan (white-label entitled), no logo uploaded yet - the upload happens on camera and the asset bundle generates from it.
- A prepared demo logo file (wordmark plus a simple mark) in the take's local upload folder. Not a real client brand.

Screen the recording opens on: `/dashboard/settings/brand`, the Brand card with the logo upload control.
Account and org: the demo studio owner session on Studio.
Recording target note: primary target is `https://staging.snaphq.app` (staging lock held per growth rule 5). This take sends no email.
Teardown:
- Remove the logo and reset the watermark mode and white-label toggle through the UI. Revert the demo org's plan if it was flipped for the take.

## Steps

| # | Caption (6 words or fewer) | Action (performer calls: move, hover, click, type, select, scroll, focus, settle, hold) | Focus element (the camera zooms on the FIRST boxed action of the step) | Approx duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Drop your logo once | `click` the logo upload label, choose the demo logo, settle on the saved state | the logo upload control | 5 s |
| 2 | The kit builds itself | `hover` the generated asset set (favicon, email header, OG card, watermark source), settle on the status line | the generated bundle status | 7 s |

Rules for every step: real clicks, typed text where a field takes it, a settle pause after each new screen.
