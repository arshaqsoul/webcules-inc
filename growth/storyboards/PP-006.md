# Storyboard: PP-006 - Photographers chase contracts and deposits for weeks before a date is truly booked

Pain point: PP-006
Persona: wedding photographer chasing signatures and retainers by email
Tier shown: free
Format: desktop
Seed: 6
Target length: 20-30 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): Still chasing signed contracts?
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is a real project's Contracts tab with a signed agreement row (status chip, signer, PDF link), so the photographer sees paperwork already closed without one email.
No logo in the first frame.

## Starting state

Fixture to seed (through the app UI and the app's own APIs, nothing by hand in the database):
- The demo org "Amara & Oak Photography", project "Daniel & Priya - Willow Creek Estate Wedding", with the seeded contract templates ("Wedding photography agreement", "Portrait session agreement") and the seeded signed wedding agreement on the same project.
- `fixture()` voids any contract a crashed earlier run left in draft or sent state for the demo client, identified by the signer name "Nora Example" in the row title, through the app's own void action.
- `fixture()` verifies the project contracts tab renders and the template select carries the wedding template.

Screen the recording opens on: `/dashboard/projects/<id>?tab=contracts`, the contracts list mid-frame, no editor open.
Account and org: the demo login from `growth/.env.local`.
Recording target note: recorded against the local dev server (`GROWTH_STAGING_URL=http://localhost:5173`), same code as staging, because staging credentials were not configured in this environment. The signing page is `/c/{token}` in the main document (no iframe). The token never appears on screen: the take reads it from the locally captured email (apps/snap/.wrangler/tmp/email, dev email worker, never delivered).

Teardown:
- `teardown()` voids the contract this run created (the signing link stops working) and verifies the row shows the void state.
- Voiding a disposable demo contract is safe; nothing else it touched persists.

## Steps

| # | Caption (6 words or fewer) | Action (performer calls: move, hover, click, type, select, scroll, focus, settle, hold) | Focus element (the camera zooms on the FIRST boxed action of the step) | Approx duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contracts live in the project | settle on the contracts tab, `move` and `hover` the signed seeded row, `hover` the "New contract" button | the signed seeded row | 4 s |
| 2 | One from a template | `click` "New contract", `select` "Wedding photography agreement" in the template picker, `hover` the filled title and body, `type` the client email, `click` "Save draft" | the template picker, then the draft row | 8 s |
| 3 | Sent, link by email | `click` "Send for signature" on this run's draft row, wait for the "Sent" notice | the send button, then the notice | 4 s |
| 4 | They sign in the browser | `navigate` to the `/c/{token}` link captured from the email, settle on the contract, `type` the full legal name, `click` "Sign contract", wait for the signed panel | the name line, then the signed panel | 7 s |
| 5 | Signed, dated, archived | `navigate` back to the contracts tab, wait for the row to read signed with the signer name and PDF link, `hover` and `focus` the row, hold | the signed row | 6 s |

Rules for every step: real clicks, typed text, eased scrolling, a settle pause after each new screen.
The contract this run creates carries "Nora Example" in its title so fixture and teardown can find exactly that row.

## The payoff shot

Step 4 into step 5: the signing page flipping to "Signed by Nora Example" with the timestamp and the "Download signed PDF" button, then, back in the project, the row's status chip reading "signed" with the signer name and PDF link.
Held for at least 1.5 s with the camera pushing in (label `payoff`).
It proves the chase is gone: the client signed in their browser and the studio saw it without one follow-up email.

## Caption draft for Instagram

Line 1 (hook, under 125 characters): Still chasing wedding clients for a signature?
Body: Build the agreement once from a template, send it from the project, and your client signs it in their browser. The signature is timestamped with their IP, archived as a PDF and mailed to both of you.
No printing, no "just checking in" emails, no lost attachments.
It is not magic: contracts work on the Free plan (two templates); Studio lifts the template limit.
Call to action (one): Send your next contract at snaphq.app
Hashtags (5 to 8, niche before broad): #weddingphotographer #photographybusiness #photographercontracts #clientcontracts #photographerworkflow #smallbusinessowner #photographycrm
