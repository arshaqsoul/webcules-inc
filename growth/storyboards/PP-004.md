# Storyboard: PP-004 - Snap launch reel, your whole studio in one tab

Pain point: PP-004
Persona: solo or small-studio wedding and portrait photographer who stitches a CRM, a gallery host, contracts and invoicing together from several tools
Tier shown: free (the staging org runs on Studio, so paid UI may be visible and stays out of every zoom)
Format: desktop
Seed: 4
Target length: 22-28 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): Juggling four tools for clients?
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is already the real Snap Overview with leads, sessions and pipeline on one screen, so the hook names the pain while the answer is visible.
No logo in the first frame.
Alternates if the editor wants one: "Your whole studio, one tab".

## Starting state

Fixture to seed: none, this take is read-only navigation, no data changes, no teardown, no email.
`fixture()` opens `/dashboard`, waits for the stat cards and the "Next sessions" panel, and throws "FIXTURE STOP" if they are missing.
Screen the recording opens on: `/dashboard` (Overview) with the stat cards, pipeline and next sessions in view, the sidebar visible, the "Studio setup" card scrolled or kept out of every focus target.
Account and org: the demo org "Amara & Oak Photography" from `growth/.env.local`, 1360x1020 desktop.
Note for QC and the recorder: staging runs this org on Studio, so Studio-only UI may be visible.
Keep it out of every zoom and never claim it.
The "Studio setup 5/11" card at the bottom of the sidebar shows on every page, so no focus target may include the lower-left sidebar.
Email: nothing is sent, do not export `sendsEmail`, the default mask blurs every address (no `allowEmails`).
Real photographer data: the seeded clients are demo people on example.net, Rice Family is used because it has no link residue.

## Steps

All navigation is by clicking sidebar items and project cards (no `page.goto` inside `run()`), so there is no white reload flash.

| # | Caption (6 words or fewer) | Action (performer calls) | Focus element | Approx duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leads, sessions, pipeline, one view | `moveTo` and `hover` the stat cards row, drift to the pipeline, then to "Next sessions" | stat cards, pipeline and next sessions panels (main area only) | 5 s |
| 2 | Every shoot becomes a project | `moveTo` and `click` sidebar link "Projects", settle 800 ms, `hover` two project cards | the projects board (do not zoom the right edge, the Complete column is clipped at 1360 px) | 5 s |
| 3 | Photos live with the job | `click` the "Rice Family" project card, settle 800 ms, `click` the "Files" tab, settle 900 ms, `hover` a photo | the Files tab photo grid (6 photos) | 6 s |
| 4 | Then get paid, right there | `click` sidebar link "Transactions", settle 900 ms, `hover` the "Collected" stat, then a succeeded row | Collected stat and the succeeded rows | 4 s |
| 5 | 0% commission, paid to you | `hold` on the Transactions screen, focus the Collected stat and the first succeeded row, label `payoff` | Collected stat plus succeeded rows | 3 s |

Rules for every step: real clicks, eased scrolling, a settle pause after each new screen, cursor never teleports.
Exact click path: Overview (start) > sidebar "Projects" > project card "Rice Family" (demo-proj-family) > "Files" tab > sidebar "Transactions".
Never click Share, Send link, Re-send, New link, Revoke, Void, Record payment, or "Hide this" on the Studio setup card.

## The payoff shot

Step 5: the Transactions screen with the "Collected" total and three succeeded rows, held at least 1.5 s with no ramp (label `payoff`).
It proves the pain is gone: clients, photos and payments were all reached from one sidebar, in one tab, with no export or second login.
The dollar amounts on screen are seeded demo data, not a plan price, and no "$" appears in the hook, captions or end card.
