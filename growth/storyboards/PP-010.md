# Storyboard: PP-010 - Clients lose scattered delivery links instead of one place to go back to

Pain point: PP-010
Persona: wedding clients who received a link once and can never find it again
Tier shown: free
Format: desktop
Seed: 10
Target length: 20-30 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): Where are the photos?
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is the client portal's login asking for just an email, the answer to every "can you resend the link?" message.
No logo in the first frame.

## Starting state

Fixture to seed (through the app UI and the app's own APIs, nothing by hand in the database):
- The demo org "Amara & Oak Photography" with the wedding project (designed gallery, signed contract, paid invoice) attached to its seeded client.
- Recording target note: the portal matches client records by email, and this environment's safe recipient is the demo client address, so the operator points the seeded wedding client row at `client@example.com` in the local database before the take and restores it after. This is environment plumbing (the same class as aligning the demo login email), not take fixture work; on a staging re-record the studio would simply use its real client's safe address.
- `fixture()` walks nothing: the take starts on the portal login itself, and the operator's flip is the only preparation.

Screen the recording opens on: `/portal`, the email step of the client login.
Account and org: no dashboard session is used anywhere in this take; the client portal session comes from the magic code.
Recording target note: recorded against the local dev server (`GROWTH_STAGING_URL=http://localhost:5173`), same code as staging. The magic code is read from the locally captured email (dev email worker, never delivered).

Teardown:
- The operator restores the seeded client email after the take. The portal session that the take minted is harmless (it is the demo client's own session).

## Steps

| # | Caption (6 words or fewer) | Action (performer calls: move, hover, click, type, select, scroll, focus, settle, hold) | Focus element (the camera zooms on the FIRST boxed action of the step) | Approx duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clients sign in by email | settle on the login, `type` the client email, `click` the send-code button, wait for the check-your-email state | the email field, then the sent state | 5 s |
| 2 | Six digits, no password | `type` the six-digit code from the email, `click` "Open my portal", settle on the dashboard | the code field, then the dashboard heading | 6 s |
| 3 | Your studios, one page | `hover` the studio section (logo line, projects and galleries stats), `hover` the signed-in line | the studio section | 5 s |
| 4 | Always there, no resend | `focus` the studio section, hold | the studio section | 4 s |

Rules for every step: real typed text, a settle pause after each new screen.

## The payoff shot

Step 2 into step 3: the code going in, then "Your studios" with the studio's logo line, its project and gallery counts, and the client's own email up in the corner.
Held for at least 1.5 s with the camera pushing in (label `payoff`).
It proves the fix: the client has one place that is always theirs, so the resend email never needs to exist.
