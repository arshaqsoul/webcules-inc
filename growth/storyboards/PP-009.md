# Storyboard: PP-009 - Clients cannot get the whole gallery because downloads fail or scare them

Pain point: PP-009
Persona: wedding clients who never get the full set their photographer delivered
Tier shown: free
Format: desktop
Seed: 9
Target length: 20-30 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): Their download failed again?
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is a real client gallery with the Download control visible, the exact place where other platforms lose the client.
No logo in the first frame.

## Starting state

Fixture to seed (through the app UI and the app's own APIs, nothing by hand in the database):
- The demo org "Amara & Oak Photography", wedding project with its designed gallery and photos.
- `fixture()` mints a fresh download-enabled grant through the app's own API (client@example.com), completes the gallery email verification once before capture, and leaves the browser on the gallery.
- `teardown()` revokes the run's grant.

Screen the recording opens on: the client gallery for the wedding project, top of the grid, Download control visible.
Account and org: the demo login from `growth/.env.local`; the client gallery session comes from the fixture's verification.
Recording target note: recorded against the local dev server (`GROWTH_STAGING_URL=http://localhost:5173`), same code as staging. The take never completes a real download; it shows the menu and the parts sheet, which is the beat.

Teardown:
- `teardown()` revokes this run's grant and verifies the row is gone.
- Revoking a disposable demo grant is safe; no download state persists.

## Steps

| # | Caption (6 words or fewer) | Action (performer calls: move, hover, click, type, select, scroll, focus, settle, hold) | Focus element (the camera zooms on the FIRST boxed action of the step) | Approx duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delivered, no Drive link | settle on the gallery, `scroll` a little so the grid reads, `hover` the Download control | the gallery grid, then the Download button | 4 s |
| 2 | One tap, everything | `click` Download, `hover` the "Download all" section and "Everything · full resolution" | the open download menu | 4 s |
| 3 | Everything, full resolution | `click` "Everything · full resolution", wait for the "Your download is starting" flash with the file count and size | the menu item, then the starting flash | 5 s |
| 4 | Ten files, on the way | `hover` and `focus` the starting flash, hold | the flash | 4 s |

Rules for every step: real clicks, eased scrolling, a settle pause after each new screen.
The take opens the download menu and picks everything, which starts the real download (ten demo photos, a few MB) - the flash is the beat and the browser download itself is never shown.

## The payoff shot

Step 3 into step 4: the "Your download is starting - 10 files, 7.1 MB." flash right after the menu pick, held with the camera pushing in.
It proves the fix: one tap and the whole gallery is already leaving, no Drive link, no resend, no zip that dies at 80 percent.
