# Storyboard: PP-012 - RAW backups are a messy afterthought that gets lost or deleted

Pain point: PP-012
Persona: wedding photographers sitting on terabytes of RAW files and a Dropbox grave they no longer trust
Tier shown: free
Format: desktop
Seed: 12
Target length: 20-30 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): Your RAW files, handled
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is the RAW Vault page with three numbers that answer the question every photographer fears: what is hot, what is cold, when does anything get deleted.
No logo in the first frame.

## What this reel honestly shows

Snap's RAW Vault is a storage lifecycle, not a client delivery tool: RAWs stay hot for 6 months, then move to cold storage, restorable in one click, never deleted without two emailed warnings. The reel says exactly that. It does NOT show or claim OTP links or client delivery of RAWs - that framing is not what the code does (raw-vault-panel and the vault page describe hot/cold storage and restore only).

## Starting state

Fixture to seed (through the app's own APIs, nothing by hand in the database):
- `GET /api/studio/raw-vault` for the summary. The take needs at least one project row with a RAW file in it (hot or cold). If every count is zero, the fixture uploads two small synthetic .DNG files (256 KB each, correct TIFF magic) into the first demo project via the app's own upload path - `POST /api/uploads`, presigned PUT, `POST /api/uploads/confirm` - and teardown bulk-deletes them. If the upload or confirm is refused, STOP with the reason. The numbers on film are always real.
- The recording login is the org owner, so the vault access gate passes.

Screen the recording opens on: `/dashboard/raw-vault`, the three summary cards (Hot storage, Cold storage, Next deletion) with the project table below.
Account and org: the staging demo org (owner login from `growth/.env.local`, programmatic).
Recording target: staging (`GROWTH_STAGING_URL=https://staging.snaphq.app`).

Teardown:
- The fixture-seeded .DNG files are bulk-deleted through `POST /api/assets/bulk` with `{ "action": "delete" }` and the deletion is verified against the vault summary. The keep-hot click itself is idempotent and stays.

## Steps

| # | Caption (6 words or fewer) | Action (performer calls: move, hover, click, type, select, scroll, focus, settle, hold) | Focus element (the camera zooms on the FIRST boxed action of the step) | Approx duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every RAW file, one vault | settle on the vault page, `hover` the Hot storage card, `hover` the Cold storage card | the Hot storage card | 6 s |
| 2 | Six months hot, then cold | `hover` the Next deletion card and its "two emailed warnings" line, `scroll` to the project table | the Next deletion card | 6 s |

Rules for every step: real hovers, a settle pause after each new screen.

## The payoff shot

Step 4: the message "restored to hot storage" (or "kept hot for 6 more months") appearing after the click, focused tight and held at least 1.5 s while the camera pushes in.
It proves the fix: nothing is silently deleted - the photographer sees the dates and pulls files back with one click.
