# Storyboard: PP-011 - Print money leaks to the platform while the photographer does the work

Pain point: PP-011
Persona: wedding photographer whose clients keep asking for prints while every platform store takes a cut
Tier shown: lite
Format: desktop
Seed: 11
Target length: 20-30 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): Print sales, 0% commission
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is the store home with the open/closed switch, the switch that turns Buy buttons on at no cut to the platform.
No logo in the first frame.

## Starting state

Fixture to seed (through the app UI and the app's own APIs, nothing by hand in the database):
- The demo org "Amara & Oak Photography" with the wedding project "Willow Creek Estate Wedding" and its delivered gallery.
- A Stripe connection on the demo org so no payouts banner crosses the frame (seeded through the app's own connect flow before the take).
- The price list, its product and the attachment are all created on camera; no store fixture exists before the take.

Screen the recording opens on: `/dashboard/store`, the store home with the store switch visible.
Account and org: the demo studio owner session on the Lite plan (staging disposable org flipped to Lite for the take, reverted after).
Recording target note: primary target is `https://staging.snaphq.app` (staging lock held per growth rule 5). This take sends no email. The address bar never shows a `snap-staging.webcules.com` URL; the watermark is `snaphq.app`.

Teardown:
- Close the store, detach the price list from the gallery, archive the price list and the product through the UI. Revert the demo org's plan after the take.

## Steps

| # | Caption (6 words or fewer) | Action (performer calls: move, hover, click, type, select, scroll, focus, settle, hold) | Focus element (the camera zooms on the FIRST boxed action of the step) | Approx duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your store, one switch | settle on the store home, `hover` the store switch, `click` it open, settle on the "Buy buttons are live" line | the store switch | 5 s |
| 2 | Prints, packages, digitals | `click` "New price list", `type` the name "Wedding Prints", in the editor `click` add product, `type` "8x10 Print", `select` the Print kind, `type` a size variant and price, settle on the product card | the kind chips (Digital, Print, Package, Custom) | 8 s |
| 3 | Markup without a spreadsheet | `hover` the bulk markup control, `type` 30, settle on the repriced variants | the markup field and the updated prices | 4 s |
| 4 | Attach it to the gallery | back on the store home, `click` attach on the wizard's last step, `select` the wedding gallery, settle on the "Attached" toast | the attach row and the toast | 5 s |
| 5 | See it as your client | `click` "Preview the store as a client", settle on the client store view in the studio's colors, `scroll` the price grid, `hover` a Buy button | the client store header in the studio accent | 6 s |

Rules for every step: real clicks, typed text, eased scrolling, a settle pause after each new screen.

## The payoff shot

Step 4 into step 5: the "Attached" toast, then the client store view wearing the studio's own colors and price list.
Held for at least 1.5 s with the camera pushing in (label `payoff`).
It proves the fix: the photographer builds the store once, attaches it to the gallery, and every dollar of a print order lands in their own Stripe account - Snap charges no commission.
