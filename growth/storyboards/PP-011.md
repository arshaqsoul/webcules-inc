# Storyboard: PP-011 - Photographers lose a commission on every gallery sale

Pain point: PP-011
Persona: wedding and portrait photographers selling prints and albums from their galleries
Tier shown: lite
Format: desktop
Seed: 11
Target length: 20-30 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): You keep 100% of sales
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is the store status card reading "Your store is open - Buy buttons are live", with the 0% commission promise as the hook line. Every other gallery platform takes a cut; this one says the quiet part on screen.
No logo in the first frame.

## Starting state

Fixture to seed (through the app's own APIs, nothing by hand in the database):
- `GET /api/studio/commerce` for the price lists. If there are none, `POST /api/studio/commerce/from-template` with `{ "template": "wedding" }` seeds the Wedding starter list atomically. If the list count is still zero after that, STOP.
- The filmed list must hold a real priced product: if no active priced print exists, the fixture POSTs one product (`8x10 print`) and one variant ($25) through the same API the editor uses, and teardown archives it.
- The take starts with the store CLOSED so the click on film is the real open: if it is open, `PATCH /api/studio/store-status` with `{ "open": false }` before capture and remember the original value for teardown.
- Verify the page is NOT the Free-plan upgrade card (the store is Lite+). If the org cannot open the store, STOP with "FIXTURE STOP: staging org is not on Lite or above".
- Note: if payouts are not connected, the setup checklist may show a payouts step. The take never zooms on it and never claims payouts are connected.

Screen the recording opens on: `/dashboard/store`, store status card on the left, price list table below.
Account and org: the staging demo org (owner login from `growth/.env.local`, programmatic).
Recording target: staging (`GROWTH_STAGING_URL=https://staging.snaphq.app`).

Teardown:
- If the take flipped the store open, PATCH the original open value back and verify.
- The Wedding price list created by the fixture stays: price lists have no delete endpoint (PATCH-only API), the same staging accumulation every prior take accepts. Re-runs reuse it.

## Steps

| # | Caption (6 words or fewer) | Action (performer calls: move, hover, click, type, select, scroll, focus, settle, hold) | Focus element (the camera zooms on the FIRST boxed action of the step) | Approx duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your store, ready when you are | settle on the store home, `hover` the status card ("Your store is closed" dot + copy) | the status card dot and headline | 5 s |
| 2 | Prints, priced once, your margins | `click` the Wedding price list link in the table, settle on the editor, `scroll` to the 8x10 print row (seeded by the fixture at $25), `hover` the row and its price | the price list link, then the print row | 8 s |
| 3 | Galleries get Buy buttons | `click` the Store sidebar link, settle on the store home, `move` to the store open toggle | the store open toggle | 5 s |
| 4 | One switch, sales on | `click` the open toggle, wait for the success toast, `focus` the toast, hold | the success toast "Store opened - Buy buttons are live on your galleries." | 5 s |

Rules for every step: real typed text where typing happens, a settle pause after each new screen.

## The payoff shot

Step 4: the toast reading "Store opened - Buy buttons are live on your galleries.", focused tight and held at least 1.5 s while the camera pushes in.
It proves the fix: flipping one switch puts Buy buttons on every gallery with an active price list, and the photographer keeps 100% of every sale.
