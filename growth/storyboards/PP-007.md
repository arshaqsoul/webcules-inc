Pain point: PP-007

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): Inquiries in five places?
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is the Leads table with a fresh "new" inquiry on top, so the photographer sees every incoming question in one list instead of five inboxes.
No logo in the first frame.

## Steps

| # | Caption (6 words or fewer) | Action (performer calls: move, hover, click, type, select, scroll, focus, settle, hold) | Focus element (the camera zooms on the FIRST boxed action of the step) | Approx duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every inquiry, one list | settle on the table, `move` and `hover` the fresh lead's row (name, email, shoot type, "new" chip) | the fresh lead row | 4 s |
| 2 | The record keeps it all | `click` the lead's name link, settle on the record (email, shoot type, event date, the inquiry message), `hover` the message | the lead record | 5 s |
| 3 | Convert in one dialog | `click` "Convert to project", `hover` the prefilled project title in the dialog, `click` "Create project" | the Convert button, then the dialog | 5 s |
| 4 | It becomes a project | wait for the green "Converted to a project" panel with the project link, `hover` and `focus` it | the converted panel | 4 s |
| 5 | Straight onto the board | `navigate` to the projects board, wait for this run's project card in Booked, `hover` and `focus` the card, hold | the new project card | 5 s |

Rules for every step: real clicks, typed text, eased scrolling, a settle pause after each new screen.
The take captures the convert response's project id and locates this run's board card by that href, so older demo cards never confuse the shot.

## The payoff shot

Step 4 into step 5: the green "Converted to a project" panel on the lead record, then the projects board with this run's card sitting in the Booked column.
Held for at least 1.5 s with the camera pushing in (label `payoff`).
It proves nothing slips: the inquiry that used to drown in DMs became a tracked, dated, booked project without a single spreadsheet row.
