# Storyboard: PP-002 - Clients take weeks to pick their photos while feedback scatters across chat and email

Pain point: PP-002
Persona: wedding photographer waiting on album or print selections
Tier shown: lite
Format: desktop
Seed: 2
Target length: 20-30 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): Still chasing photo picks?
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is the real Client gallery panel showing a link row that reads "Awaiting selection (max 5)" with the due date beside it, so the photographer sees their own waiting-for-them moment and where the picks will land.
No logo in the first frame.

## Starting state

Fixture to seed (through the app UI and the app's own APIs, nothing by hand in the database):
- The demo org "Amara & Oak Photography", project "Autumn Winds Shoot", 10 approved photos, designed gallery ("Anna & Benjamin" cover).
- `fixture()` opens the dashboard, resolves the project link by title, then mints a fresh share grant through the app's own API from the logged-in page: POST `/api/projects/{id}/grants` with clientEmail client@example.com, selectionMode "selection", selectionLimit 5, selectionDeadline 7 days out, downloads on. The response carries the client gallery URL, which the take opens in step 2. Minting the grant emails the demo client (captured to disk on the local target, never delivered).
- The gallery write APIs need the gallery session cookie, which only an email verification mints. `fixture()` completes that verification once before capture, exactly as a real client's first open does: enter the address on the gallery's gate, the 6-digit code arrives in the local email capture, enter it, the gallery opens. The reel itself then starts straight on the gallery, like every later client visit.
- `fixture()` navigates to `?tab=gallery`, verifies exactly one awaiting-selection row for the demo client (the fresh grant), and scrolls it mid-screen.
- Older grants from earlier takes are revoked by their own teardown, so they never appear as live rows.

Screen the recording opens on: `/dashboard/projects/<id>?tab=gallery`, the awaiting row in the middle of the frame, no flash banner showing.
Account and org: the demo login from `growth/.env.local`.
Recording target note: recorded against the local dev server (`GROWTH_STAGING_URL=http://localhost:5173`) because staging credentials were not available in this environment. Same code as staging. On a staging re-record, `CLIENT` must change to that environment's `GROWTH_SAFE_EMAILS` address and the OTP gate must be off for the client link to open in the take.

Teardown:
- `teardown()` revokes the grant this run minted (POST `/api/grants/{id}/revoke` from the logged-in page) and verifies the row shows the revoked state, so the next run starts with exactly one live row again.
- Revoking a disposable demo grant is safe; the picks data on it is demo data.
- The submitted selection itself is left as-is: it belongs to the revoked grant.

## Steps

| # | Caption (6 words or fewer) | Action (performer calls) | Focus element | Approx duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | You asked for 5 picks | settle on the gallery tab, `moveTo` and `hover` the awaiting row (chip, client email, "Awaiting selection (max 5)" with the due date) | the awaiting row with the selection status line | 4 s |
| 2 | They open the link | `navigate` to the client gallery URL from the fixture grant, settle on the hero, `scroll` to the photo grid | the gallery hero, then the grid | 5 s |
| 3 | Picks take three taps | `moveTo` and `click` the pick check on three different photos; after each click the bottom dock counter grows ("1 picked of 5 allowed" and so on), `hover` the counter | first photo's pick control, then the dock counter | 8 s |
| 4 | Sent from the gallery | `moveTo` and `click` "Send selection" in the dock, wait for the "Selection sent to your photographer" flash | the "Send selection" button, then the flash | 4 s |
| 5 | They land in your project | `navigate` back to the project gallery tab, wait for the row to read "Selection in: 3 picked of 5", `click` "View picks", settle on the picks panel | the updated row, then the picks panel | 6 s |

Rules for every step: real clicks, eased scrolling, a settle pause after each new screen.
Pick controls are located by their stable aria-label, which reads "Add" plus the photo filename plus "to selection"; after a photo is picked its control becomes "Remove", so always take the first "Add" control for the next tap.

## The payoff shot

Step 4 into step 5: the flash "Selection sent to your photographer" on the client gallery, then, back in the project, the row reading "Selection in: 3 picked of 5" and the picks panel showing exactly the three chosen photos.
Held for at least 1.5 s with the camera pushing in (label `payoff`).
It proves the chase is gone: the client picked in the gallery itself and the selection arrived in the project without a single chat message.

## Caption draft for Instagram

Line 1 (hook, under 125 characters): The album is designed and your client will not pick photos.
Body: Share the gallery in selection mode: the client taps a check on the photos they want, sees their own counter against your limit, and sends the picks from the gallery itself. No chat screenshots, no filename lists.
The selection lands straight on the gallery row in your project, and View picks shows exactly what they chose. Set a deadline so the date is on the screen while they browse.
It is not magic: selection mode is on every plan; the nudge button and favorites exports start on Lite and Studio.
Call to action (one): Let them pick at snaphq.app
Hashtags (5 to 8, niche before broad): #weddingphotographer #photographybusiness #clientgallery #albumdesign #photographerworkflow #photographytips #proofing
