# Storyboard: PP-003 - Gallery links get forwarded and photographers cannot control who has access

Pain point: PP-003
Persona: wedding photographer who delivers online galleries and worries a client forwarded the link
Tier shown: free
Format: desktop
Seed: 3
Target length: 15-25 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): Client forwarded your gallery link?
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is the real "Client gallery" panel with an ACTIVE link row, so the photographer sees their own worry and the control for it at once.
No logo in the first frame.

## Starting state

Fixture to seed (all through the app UI, nothing by hand in the database):
- The org has the demo project `demo-proj-wedding` with exactly one share link row under "Client gallery", sent to an obviously fake client email (a `@example.com` demo address already in staging).
- That row is in the ACTIVE state with the expiry select on "No expiry".
- `fixture()` opens `/dashboard/projects/demo-proj-wedding?tab=gallery`, reads the row, and if the expiry select is not "No expiry" it sets it back to "No expiry" with the real select.
- `fixture()` verifies the row chip reads ACTIVE.
  If the row is Revoked or Expired (no "New link" button), the recorder must stop and block the record, because the app has no way to un-revoke a link.
  Never run a take that clicks "Revoke" on the seeded link.
- Do not expand the row, do not open the Activity panel, do not scroll to download PIN or watermark settings.

Screen the recording opens on: `/dashboard/projects/demo-proj-wedding?tab=gallery`, scrolled so the "Client gallery" heading and the link row are both visible, no flash banner showing.
Account and org: the demo org from `growth/.env.local`.
Note for QC and the recorder: staging runs this org on Studio, so Studio-only controls may be visible on the page.
They must stay out of frame and out of every zoom.

Teardown (via the app UI, after the take):
- "New link" creates a new ACTIVE grant that carries the expiry over (see `app/api/grants/[id]/regenerate/route.ts`), so the project still shows exactly one ACTIVE link row afterwards and the next take can run unchanged.
- `teardown()` reopens `?tab=gallery` and sets the expiry select back to "No expiry" with the real select, then confirms the row shows "no expiry" and the ACTIVE chip.
- `teardown()` must not click "Revoke".
- Regeneration emails the demo address only, which is a disposable staging address.
  Do not change the client email to any real address.

## Steps

| # | Caption (6 words or fewer) | Action (performer calls) | Focus element | Approx duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Link already sent to a client | `navigate` to the gallery tab, settle 700 ms, `moveTo` the link row, `hover` the ACTIVE chip and client email | link row with ACTIVE chip and client email | 3 s |
| 2 | Set it to expire | `moveTo` and `click` the "Edit expiry" select, `click` the option "7 days", settle until the row text reads "expires" with a date | select with aria-label "Edit expiry" | 4 s |
| 3 | Or kill the old link | `moveTo` and `hover` the "New link" button 250 ms, `click` it | button "New link" | 3 s |
| 4 | Confirm, the old one dies | confirm dialog "Are you sure?" appears, settle 700 ms so the text is readable, `click` the confirm button | confirm dialog body "The old link stops working immediately" | 4 s |
| 5 | Fresh link, old one dead | wait for the "Link emailed to the client." banner with the new URL, hold | flash banner with the new URL and the ACTIVE row | 4 s |

Rules for every step: real clicks, typed text (none needed here), eased scrolling, a settle pause after each new screen.
Pick the option in step 2 with real pointer actions on the select, not a programmatic set.

## The payoff shot

Step 5: the banner "Link emailed to the client." with the new URL, the row still ACTIVE, and the expiry now showing the 7 day expiry date.
Held for at least 1.5 s with no motion required (the editor must not ramp it, label it `payoff`).
It proves the photographer controlled access in two clicks: the old forwarded URL no longer works and the new one reached only the client.

## Do not show

- The Activity panel, views, favorites or last-seen text (Lite and above).
- Download PIN, watermarks, deterrents, approvals, guests or schedule (Lite and above, Studio and above).
- The "Preview" button (it opens the client view without a code and would mislead).
- `/dashboard/galleries`.
- The "Revoke" button clicked, and any other plan name, price or limit.
- Any real client name or email, the browser address bar, and the full email text of the link beyond the demo URL.
- Any claim that forwarding is impossible or the gallery is fully secure.
