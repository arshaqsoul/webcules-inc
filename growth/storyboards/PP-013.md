# Storyboard: PP-013 - Studios with two or more photographers run on one shared login

Pain point: PP-013
Persona: lead photographer sharing one password with a second shooter because no studio tool has real seats
Tier shown: studio
Format: desktop
Seed: 13
Target length: 20-30 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): Two photographers, one login?
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is Settings - Team with the seats counter, the page no photographer CRM has.
No logo in the first frame.

## Starting state

Fixture to seed (through the app UI and the app's own APIs, nothing by hand in the database):
- The demo org "Amara & Oak Photography" on the Studio plan (3 seats), the owner session only. No second member exists before the take.
- The invite recipient is the demo second-shooter address `second@example.com`, which must be listed in the recording environment's `GROWTH_SAFE_EMAILS`. The founder's real gmail is never used anywhere in this take.

Screen the recording opens on: `/dashboard/settings/team`, the Team card with the seats counter.
Account and org: the demo studio owner session.
Recording target note: primary target is `https://staging.snaphq.app` (staging lock held per growth rule 5). The take sends one invite email to the safe address above; the inbox is never opened on screen - the on-screen proof is the "Invite sent" notice and the pending-invites row.
Teardown:
- Cancel the pending invite through the UI after the take. Revert the demo org's plan if it was flipped for the take.
