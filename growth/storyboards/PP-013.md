# Storyboard: PP-013 - Studios run multiple photographers through one shared login

Pain point: PP-013
Persona: studio owners juggling second shooters over one shared email and password
Tier shown: pro
Format: desktop
Seed: 13
Target length: 20-30 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): Two shooters, one studio
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is the team page with real roles - Owner, Admin, Member - instead of the one-login-everyone-shares reality most studio CRMs offer.
No logo in the first frame.

## Starting state

Fixture to seed (through the app's own APIs and page UI, nothing by hand in the database):
- Open `/dashboard/settings/team` as the org owner (from `growth/.env.local`). If the invite form is missing because every seat is taken, STOP with "FIXTURE STOP: no free seats on the staging org - remove a test member first".
- If a pending invite to the safe address already exists (a previous take), cancel it via the row's Cancel button, auto-accepting the confirm dialog, so the take is repeatable.
- The invite is the ONLY email this take sends, and it goes to the one address in `GROWTH_SAFE_EMAILS`.

Screen the recording opens on: `/dashboard/settings/team`, member list with role chips, the invite form below.
Account and org: the staging demo org, owner login, programmatic.
Recording target: staging (`GROWTH_STAGING_URL=https://staging.snaphq.app`).

Teardown:
- Cancel the pending invite to the safe address (confirm dialog auto-accepted) and verify the pending list no longer shows it. The invite email may still arrive at the safe address; nobody reads it.

## The payoff shot

Step 3: the green notice "Invite sent to (safe address) - it expires in 7 days.", focused tight and held at least 1.5 s while the camera pushes in.
It proves the fix: a second shooter gets their own login with member limits, not your password.
