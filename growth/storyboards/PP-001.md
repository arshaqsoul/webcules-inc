# Storyboard: PP-001 - Photographers chase late client payments through awkward email threads

Pain point: PP-001
Persona: wedding photographer waiting on a deposit or final balance
Tier shown: lite
Format: desktop
Seed: 1
Target length: 20-30 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): Chasing the deposit again?
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is the real Payments & invoices panel of a booked wedding project, so the photographer sees their own unpaid-balance moment and the fix for it at once.
No logo in the first frame.

## Starting state

Fixture to seed (all through the app UI and the app's own APIs, nothing by hand in the database):
- The demo org "Amara & Oak Photography" has the booked project "Autumn Winds Shoot" with the client "Nora & Elias Example" (client@example.com, a @example.com demo address).
- `fixture()` opens the dashboard, finds the project link by its title, and navigates to its `?tab=payments` page.
- `fixture()` verifies the "New invoice" button is present and that the invoice list has no live draft, so the take always creates a fresh row.
- The org already has the "Wedding collection" invoice preset (three itemized lines). If the preset select does not appear when the composer opens, the take stops: seed the preset in Templates first.
- The package quote is set to $3,200 so the composer's amount field is prefilled; the preset overrides it.

Screen the recording opens on: `/dashboard/projects/<id>?tab=payments`, scrolled to the Invoices panel, no notice text showing.
Account and org: the demo login from `growth/.env.local`.
Recording target note: this reel is recorded against the local dev server (`GROWTH_STAGING_URL=http://localhost:5173`), because staging credentials were not available in this environment. The flows are the same code as staging; if it is re-recorded on staging, `CLIENT` must change to an address in that environment's `GROWTH_SAFE_EMAILS` (a @example.com address would bounce there).

Teardown:
- None required. The take creates a new invoice each run (rows accumulate, which is harmless demo data like PP-003's history rows).
- `teardown()` verifies the last row reads "paid" and leaves it in place. It must not click "Void" on any row.

## Steps

| # | Caption (6 words or fewer) | Action (performer calls) | Focus element | Approx duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open a new invoice | `moveTo` and `click` the "New invoice" button, settle until the composer form is visible, hover the composer | "New invoice" button, then the composer panel | 4 s |
| 2 | Apply the package | `moveTo` the "Apply a package preset" select, `select` "Wedding collection", wait for the itemized lines, `focus` the lines | preset select, then the itemized lines list | 6 s |
| 3 | Create the draft | `moveTo` and `click` "Create draft", settle until the "Draft created" notice and the draft row appear, `focus` the draft row | the draft row with the "draft" chip | 5 s |
| 4 | Send the secure link | `moveTo`, `hover`, `click` "Send" on that same row, wait for the "Invoice sent" banner | the row's "Send" button, then the sent banner | 5 s |
| 5 | Settled, no chase | `moveTo` and `click` "Mark paid", settle so the "Mark paid?" dialog is readable, `click` "Confirm", wait for the paid chip | the confirm dialog, then the "paid" chip | 6 s |

Rules for every step: real clicks, eased scrolling, a settle pause after each new screen.
The draft row locator is scoped to the row containing the demo client email, because the list can accumulate rows from earlier takes.

## The payoff shot

Step 5: the confirm dialog, then the row flipping to the green "paid" chip with the invoice number and amount beside it.
Held for at least 1.5 s with the camera pushing in (label `payoff`).
It proves the chase is gone: the invoice went out with a secure link and the money is marked settled without a single awkward email.
