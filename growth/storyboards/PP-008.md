# Storyboard: PP-008 - Clients see the platform brand on the photographer galleries

Pain point: PP-008
Persona: wedding photographer whose clients land on a platform wordmark
Tier shown: studio
Format: desktop
Seed: 8
Target length: 20-30 s

## Hook (first 1.5 s)

On-screen text (6 words or fewer): Their brand on your gallery?
Why it stops the scroll: the first frame is a real client gallery footer with the platform badge on it, the exact thing photographers pay to remove elsewhere.
No logo in the first frame.

## Starting state

Fixture to seed (through the app UI and the app's own APIs, nothing by hand in the database):
- The demo org "Amara & Oak Photography" is on the Studio plan, so white-label is entitled; the toggle starts OFF (removeBranding false), which is the before state.
- `fixture()` mints a fresh client gallery grant on the wedding project through the app's own API (client@example.com, downloads on), completes the gallery email verification once before capture the way a real client's first open does, and leaves the browser on the client gallery footer.
- `fixture()` verifies the footer shows the platform badge (the before state) and that the brand settings page carries the white-label card.
- `teardown()` revokes the run's grant and flips the toggle back off, so the next run sees the same before state.

Screen the recording opens on: the client gallery for the wedding project, scrolled to the footer badge.
Account and org: the demo login from `growth/.env.local`; the client gallery session comes from the fixture's verification.
Recording target note: recorded against the local dev server (`GROWTH_STAGING_URL=http://localhost:5173`), same code as staging. The toggle lives on the dashboard brand settings page; the badge lives on the client gallery. Both are main-document pages.

Teardown:
- `teardown()` PUTs removeBranding false (the same endpoint the card uses) and verifies the toggle reads off again, then revokes this run's grant.
- Flipping a demo toggle and revoking a demo grant touches nothing that matters.

## Steps

| # | Caption (6 words or fewer) | Action (performer calls: move, hover, click, type, select, scroll, focus, settle, hold) | Focus element (the camera zooms on the FIRST boxed action of the step) | Approx duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Spot the extra brand | settle on the client gallery, `scroll` to the footer, `hover` and `focus` the platform badge | the Delivered-by badge | 5 s |
| 2 | Studio unlocks the flip | `navigate` to the dashboard brand settings, `hover` the white-label card and its before/after table | the white-label card | 5 s |
| 3 | Flip it once | `click` the "Remove Snap branding" toggle, wait for the saved state, `hover` the card | the toggle | 4 s |
| 4 | Now it is all yours | `navigate` back to the same client gallery, `scroll` to the footer, `hover` and `focus` the studio copyright line | the studio copyright footer | 5 s |

Rules for every step: real clicks, eased scrolling, a settle pause after each new screen.

## The payoff shot

Step 3 into step 4: the toggle flipping on, then the same gallery footer now reading "© Amara & Oak Photography" with no platform badge anywhere.
Held for at least 1.5 s with the camera pushing in (label `payoff`).
It proves the fix is one switch: the client sees the studio, not the tool.
