## What the board decides

- **Palette by hour**: navy and one amber window (pre-dawn), slate with pink (blue hour), peach mist (sunrise), green and gold shafts (falls), peach haze (golden hour), indigo with warm string lights (night). Every chapter has one warm source against a cool world.
- **The van is the thread**: a rounded teal pop-top camper with an amber interior appears in the diorama, at blue hour, on the tailgate and under the stars, always derived from one picked keyframe.
- **Gestures**: tripod low on a rock, crouched in grass with the camera up, the couple laughing in the spray, the spin on the cliff, the mug to the sky.
