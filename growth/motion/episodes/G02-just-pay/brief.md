# G02 I wish they'd just pay - director's brief

Glass Studio register, wish format, feature: invoices paid online into your own Stripe (invoice.paid). See growth/motion/INSTAGRAM.md.

The fall family from S01 stands with the photographer in the lavender glass studio after their shoot.
She has the look every photographer knows: the session was lovely, now comes the awkward part.
The cover says it for her: "I wish they'd just *pay*."
A soft puff of lavender light rises from the family; a ribbon of iridescent glass light, the invoice ribbon from the grid's invoice poster, swirls across to her and settles as a glowing glass coin in her open hand. She laughs.
The Snap inbox card drops in: "Invoice INV-0042 paid", "into your own Stripe".

Feels light, a little magical, a weight lifted. The honest line lives in the caption: you still send the invoice; they pay it online in one click.
