Vertical frame, the whole island in view with sky around it. a small round floating island of autumn park drifting in the sky above soft clouds: a grassy top carpeted with fallen orange and yellow leaves, four sculpted autumn maple and oak trees in orange, red and gold, a red and white gingham picnic blanket with a wicker basket, a few small pumpkins, the island's underside a craggy rock and soil cone with hanging roots. Lighting plan: 1 PM autumn sun high from the upper left, warm 4500 K, dappled leaf shadows on the blanket, soft fill from the pale blue sky. Camera: 45 mm tilt-shift macro lens at f/2.8, elevated three-quarter view looking down about 30 degrees. Standing on the leaf-covered grass of the same island, a few steps in front of the blanket, at the same small figurine scale as the family, is the photographer: a 41-year-old woman, 175 cm, 70 kg, slim, salt-and-pepper hair in a low bun, rectangular black glasses, charcoal quilted gilet over an oatmeal roll-neck sweater, olive canvas field trousers, brown hiking boots, holding a weathered tan mirrorless camera with a silver top plate and a worn brown leather strap up to her eye, seen from behind three-quarters as she shoots the family. Everyone stands on the island; nothing stands in the air. A few falling leaves hang in the air.
