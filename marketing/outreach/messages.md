# Outreach messages

Rules for every message:
- Say one true, specific thing about their business. Open their site on your phone first and look.
- Send 10 a day at most, by hand, one at a time. Never paste the same text to a list.
- Follow up twice (day 3 and day 7), then stop.
- Anyone who says no, or does not want to be contacted, goes to `status = do not contact` and never hears from you again.
- Email in Canada falls under CASL: send only to a business address that is published publicly for business purposes, make it relevant to their role, sign with your name and phone, and include the opt-out line.

## What to notice (only use what is true)
- Site does not work well on a phone
- No way to book or enquire online
- Site looks like it has not been updated in years
- No website, only a Facebook page
- Slow to load, or contact details hard to find

"Hi, is the owner or manager around? I'm Arshaq, a web developer in Saskatoon. I looked at your website and had a quick idea. I'm building 5 founding-client sites at a reduced price in exchange for a testimonial. Could I send you the details, or book 15 minutes?"

## 6. When they reply "interested"
Reply within the hour. Offer two time slots and the Cal link. On the call: ask what they want the website to do, how clients reach them now, and what is not working. Do not quote until you have the scope. Send the founding-client scope sheet after.
